# Accumulated Visual Reference Profile

Treat this as an aesthetic and realism calibration set, not an identity profile. Do not infer ethnicity, biography, personality, or health. Several images are heavily filtered, reposted, duplicated, composited, or captured from a video interface.

## Dataset calibration

The expanded batch contains close selfies, bedroom portraits, café moments, outdoor environmental portraits, a street-motion image, stylized studio/editorial images, a comparison collage, and a video screenshot. Two pairs repeat the same frame. Deduplicate repeated frames so they do not receive extra weight.

Classify the useful signal:

- strongest candid/lived-in evidence: café table, outdoor table setting, stone steps, bedding, desk/reading position, street laugh, and the more spontaneous side of the comparison collage;
- curated but physically grounded: lakeside dock, seated waterside portrait, window-side couch, vanity pose, and field portrait;
- styling-only or weak realism evidence: high-retouch close selfies, staged bedroom glamour, cat-ear selfie, hair-spread studio portrait, and the strongly filtered video screenshot;
- non-image artifacts: captions, comparison labels, platform marks, video controls, status bar, white borders, and repost watermarks. Never reproduce them unless explicitly requested.

## Recurring visible aesthetic

Use only when the user wants the accumulated sample aesthetic rather than a new subject design.

- Dark medium-to-long hair, often with curtain fringe, wispy bangs, loose ponytails, waves, or slightly disordered crowns.
- Soft oval-to-tapered face presentation, delicate brows, emphasized eyes, restrained lip color, and light makeup.
- Neutral, cream, white, black, pale blue, denim, muted green, and brown clothing recur.
- Styling ranges from soft domestic and preppy casual to restrained feminine editorial.

Do not reproduce the recurring enlarged-eye, narrowed-jaw, whitened-skin, or synthetic-iris effect seen in some filtered samples. Restore plausible face volume, eye scale, local skin tone, and lens-consistent geometry.

## What creates the strongest lived-in quality

### 1. An unfinished action

- Holding a drink, resting a chin, turning toward something outside frame, adjusting hair, shielding the face, reading, laughing mid-step, or settling into bedding.
- The gesture appears to have a before and after; it is not a pose assembled only for the camera.

### 2. Physical contact and weight

- A cheek compresses into a hand or pillow.
- A forearm supports the torso on a table, couch, step, or floor.
- Backpack straps, collars, bedding, hems, and chair edges alter fabric and posture.
- Bent knees, crossed ankles, shifted hips, and planted hands visibly manage balance.

### 3. Partial information

- Hair, fingers, sleeves, a flower, bedding, or a hand partly obscures the face.
- Crops omit part of the body or place the subject away from center.
- Gaze often lands just beside the lens, toward a companion, window, or passing event.

### 4. Environment that participates

- Cups, a straw, tableware, flowers, wine bottle, handbag, book, pillow, railing, stairs, camera, water, grass, and window light support the action.
- Backgrounds are ordinary enough to contain seams, clutter, passersby, uneven plants, furniture edges, or architecture that was not arranged around the subject.

### 5. Causal camera character

- Close phone selfies show mild wide-angle perspective and informal cropping.
- Indoor frames show warm mixed light, shadow noise, or limited dynamic range.
- Bright outdoor frames permit hard shadows or locally clipped highlights.
- Street action permits background motion softness while the body remains readable.
- Reposted low-resolution images may show compression, but do not imitate blur everywhere.

## Lighting and atmosphere clusters

### Domestic window light

Soft or directional daylight enters from one visible side, falls across bedding, wood, skin, and fabric, then weakens naturally. Use warm cream neutrals, quiet posture, close distance, and soft material compression.

### Warm café or evening interior

Warm practical light mixes with dim ambient fill. Preserve deeper shadows, slight color variation, ordinary table objects, and a relaxed incomplete expression.

### Hard outdoor daylight

Use crisp cast shadows, narrowed eyes or head turns away from glare, brighter shoulders and hair edges, and believable ground or architecture reflections. Do not combine hard sun with completely shadowless skin.

### Open shade and waterside air

Use cool-to-neutral daylight, lower contrast, environmental green/blue influence, mild wind response in hair and fabric, and enough depth to locate the subject in the setting.

### Editorial or studio light

Use only when requested. Keep the deliberate silhouette and styling, but do not label it candid. Preserve real hair grouping, skin material, body support, and a physically identifiable key light.

## Composition families

- intimate phone close-up: high or slightly off-axis camera, face not perfectly centered, one hand or strand near the face;
- medium domestic portrait: body leaning or seated, soft foreground material, window or lamp motivating the light;
- environmental full/three-quarter portrait: subject smaller in frame, architecture or landscape retained, natural balance and contact;
- dynamic street frame: mid-action gesture, directional gaze, slight background blur, practical bag or clothing movement;
- curated editorial: cleaner silhouette and stronger styling, explicitly acknowledged as staged.

## Freshness formula

`lived-in quality = unfinished action × physical contact × scene evidence × mild capture limitation × restrained styling`

Keep beauty and realism compatible. The subject may be attractive, well dressed, and carefully lit while still retaining plausible eye scale, face volume, skin variation, hair grouping, joint loading, fabric pressure, and environmental response.

## Reusable sample-aligned prompt

`A photorealistic lifestyle portrait of an attractive woman caught in a small unfinished everyday action, not holding a finished model pose. Her weight is visibly supported by the chair, table, pillow, step, wall, or ground; one hand interacts naturally with an ordinary object and lightly changes the nearby fabric or skin. Dark hair falls in grouped layers with a few displaced strands, light makeup remains integrated with varied skin tone and restrained texture, and the eyes keep plausible human scale and imperfect symmetry. Place her in an ordinary but visually pleasing café, bedroom, street, waterside path, or sunlit interior with concrete narrative evidence. Use a physically identifiable light source, off-center or slightly occluded composition, natural focus falloff, and one mild phone-camera or handheld limitation. Preserve attractiveness without digital face slimming, enlarged irises, waxy skin, synthetic catchlights, captions, UI, or watermarks.`

Adapt subject appearance to the user's request. Borrow the samples' moment design and physical credibility without combining every hairstyle, setting, prop, and mood into one image.
